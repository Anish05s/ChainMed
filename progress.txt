========================================================================
CHAINMED (PHARMACHAIN) - PROJECT OVERVIEW & FEATURES
========================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
ChainMed is a secure supply chain verification platform for pharmaceuticals.
It uses a three-party attestation model (Manufacturer + Supplier + Hospital)
combined with Hybrid AI and Blockchain to detect fraud and trace medicines
without requiring expensive IoT hardware.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE CONCEPT: THREE-PARTY ATTESTATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To verify a shipment, all three parties must independently report details:
- Manufacturer: Dispatches the batch with specific details.
- Supplier: Verifies the received shipment and dispatches it.
- Hospital/NGO: Confirms final receipt at the clinic.

If any party's report does not match (e.g., quantities, expiry dates, or 
temperatures differ), the system flags the batch as high-risk.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Hybrid AI Verification Engine
  - Layer 1 (Rule Engine): Automatically computes a mathematical risk score 
    based on quantity deviations, expiry mismatches, and temperature breaches.
  - Layer 2 (Gemini LLM): If a shipment is flagged, Google Gemini generates 
    a detailed plain-English investigation report for compliance officers.

* Cryptographic Security & Identity (ECDSA)
  - Implements server-side ECDSA (secp256k1) signatures for all entities 
    (Manufacturers, Suppliers, Hospitals, Admins, and Backend System).
  - Provides cryptographic proof of origin and non-repudiation.

* Privacy-Preserving Quantity Validation (ZKP Layer)
  - Uses SHA-256 salted hash commitments (Zero-Knowledge Proof concept) to 
    verify quantities across the supply chain without exposing raw stock numbers.

* Ethereum Blockchain Integration
  - Records cryptographic hashes of approvals, handoffs, and fraud flags 
    immutably on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet (with a local mock fallback).

* Crisis Detection & Rerouting AI
  - Monitors real-world supply chain disruptions (via GDELT + OpenWeather).
  - Uses Dijkstra's algorithm to suggest optimized alternate transit routes.

* Stock & Inventory Intelligence
  - Tracks stock levels at clinics and auto-generates restock requests when 
    inventories fall below safety thresholds.

* Dynamic Trust Engine
  - Adjusts trust scores for Manufacturers, Suppliers, and Hospitals based on 
    their history of successful match-ups and verification behaviors.

* Public QR Code Verification
  - Generates secure QR codes for packaging so anyone can scan and verify 
    the medicine's authentic history.
